# ADDISON — The Bulletin Board

Two pools. **Ambient** rotates randomly. **Story** is fixed and linear.

The single best property of a bulletin board is that things get *removed*. A notice the player saw yesterday is gone today. That costs you two integers — `posted_day` and `pulled_day` — and it does more atmospheric work than anything else in this document.

---

# Mechanics

**Board holds 4 slots.** Three ambient, one story. Story postings take priority and hold their slot until pulled.

**Two integers per posting** — `posted_day`, `pulled_day`. That's the whole system.

**Pulled postings should be missable.** A one-day window on Days 6, 11, 12, 13, and 16 means an inattentive player gets a thinner Mercer thread. That's correct — it rewards the same attentiveness the inspection loop rewards, and it means the board isn't just decoration.

**Visiting costs daylight.** Two minutes. It competes with inspections, so reading the board is a real choice, same as everything else.

**Late game: you can post.** Unlocks Act III. Posting the variance record is the mechanical form of the whistleblower ending — you walk to the board and pin it up, and it stays up through Day 20.

**Pool A never references the story.** Not once. The Mercer thread is legible only because everything around it is noise about brisket and Pablo.
